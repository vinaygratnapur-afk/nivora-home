GOOD TO KNOW

A few things, made clear.

Is my appointment confirmed when I submit a request?

No. Your request is an enquiry. We first check availability and share the final quotation. Your appointment is confirmed only after you agree.

Do I pay online on this website?

Not currently. Nivora is operating as a booking and partner-management pilot. No online payment is collected through this website.

How do I check my request status?

Use Track booking with the reference number and mobile number you provided when submitting your request.

Will every service be available in my area?

Availability depends on the service, partner coverage and preferred time. Our team checks these details after receiving your request.
